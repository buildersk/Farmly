Farmly - Refunds

Last updated: 26 November 2026

All purchases go through Google Play, and Google Play's refund rules apply.

Within 48 hours

Ask Google directly: on play.google.com or in the Play Store app open your profile > Payments and subscriptions > Budget and history, choose the purchase and tap "Report a problem". Google usually decides within one working day.

After 48 hours, or if something went wrong

Write to farmly.support@gmail.com with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA). If you paid and did not receive the item, or bought something by mistake and did not use it, we will fix it or refund it.

A purchase is missing

First try Settings > Restore purchases. If it is still missing, write to us with the order number and we will add it.

A charge you do not recognise

If someone used your Google account to buy without asking, report it to Google from the same Budget and history page as soon as you can (Google accepts these reports for up to 120 days). Parents can turn on purchase approval for a child's account in Google Family Link.

After a refund

When a purchase is refunded, the items from that purchase may be taken back from the farm.

If you live in the EU or UK, your legal consumer rights are not limited by this page.
