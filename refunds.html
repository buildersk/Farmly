Farmly - Refunds

Last updated: 12 November 2026

All purchases go through Google Play, and Google Play's refund rules apply.

Within 48 hours

Ask Google directly: open Google Play > your profile > Payments and subscriptions > Budget and history, choose the purchase and tap "Report a problem".

After 48 hours, or if something went wrong

Write to farmly.support@gmail.com with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA). If you paid and did not receive the item, or bought something by mistake and did not use it, we will fix it or refund it.

A purchase is missing

First try Settings > Restore purchases.

If you live in the EU or UK, your legal consumer rights are not limited by this page.
