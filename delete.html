Farmly - Delete my data

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how to delete the data the game Farmly by MatusBuilder stores about you.

In the game (fastest)

Open Farmly > Settings (the gear) > Delete my data > Yes. This erases at once: the farm on your phone, the copy of your farm in the cloud, and your random player number. The game then starts from the beginning.

If the game is no longer installed

Write to farmly.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Please add your support code (it is shown in the game under Settings > Cloud save), or tell us that your farm was connected to Google Play Games. We delete your cloud copy and player number within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your farm (level, coins, buildings, items), your nickname and farm name, your friend code and friends list, the random player number, and the link to Google Play Games if you made one.

What is not deleted by us

Records of purchases are kept by Google Play under Google's own rules, so bought passes can still be restored. Data held by ad networks is handled under their own policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Emails you sent us are deleted after two years.

Delete only part of the data

There is nothing else stored that could be deleted separately: Farmly does not keep a name, email, photos or contacts.
