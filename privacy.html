Farmly - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Farmly is a game made by lopattko ("we"), the developer named on Farmly's Google Play page, who is responsible for your data. This page says what the game stores about you, why, and how you can delete it. Contact: farmly.support@gmail.com

In short

No real name, no email, no password, no chat. The game keeps your farm on your phone and a copy online under a random player number, shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for a reward, and sells optional items through Google Play. You can delete everything with one button.

What the game stores

Your farm: level, coins, buildings, items and passes you own, settings, and the date you last played.

A random player number and a random number for this install, both made by the game. They are not your phone's hardware ID and do not say who you are.

A nickname and a name for your farm, which you choose on the first start. The nickname is also your friend code and cannot be changed afterwards; the farm can be renamed in Settings. Please do not use your real name. Players under 16 pick from ready-made names instead of typing. Rude names are refused.

Your answer to the age question on the first start. You slide to your age, but the game keeps only whether you are under 13, under 16 or older, never the age itself.

If you connect Google Play Games: your Play Games player ID, so the farm can follow you to a new phone. We do not receive your Google name, email, photo or friends.

Cloud save

A copy of your farm is kept online so it is not lost. The game signs in by itself and stores the copy under the random player number with Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Cloud Save), run by Unity Technologies. When the game talks to these servers, Unity also sees technical data every internet service sees, such as your IP address and the type of device. Unity works for us as a service provider and may store the data in the European Union or the United States. Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Friends

Your friend code is your nickname plus a few digits that the game's server adds so that it is yours alone, for example Robin#12345. The nickname is stored with Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Friends) together with your random player number. If you give your code to someone, they can ask to be your friend; nothing is shown until you say yes. Friends see each other's nickname, farm name, level, coins earned and when they last played, and can visit each other's farm to look at it: its place, buildings, decorations and field. Nothing else is shown, and a visitor cannot change anything on your farm. You can remove a friend at any time and report a name that breaks the rules from the Friends window.

Reminders and sharing

If you allow it, the game shows one reminder a day when your daily gift is ready. The reminder is made on your phone; nothing is sent to us for it, and you can switch it off in Settings. A farm postcard is a picture of your farm that is saved to your phone's pictures and shared only when you choose to share it, with the app you pick.

Phone backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Google keeps a copy of the game's data in your own Google account. We cannot see that copy.

Ads

Farmly is free. There are no ad breaks: an ad only plays when you choose to watch one for a free reward, and the game can be played without internet and without ads. The ads come from our ad partner Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. To show an ad, measure it and stop fraud, they receive your device type, language, rough region taken from the IP address and how you interact with the ad. Personalised ads are off until you switch them on yourself in Settings > Ad choices, which only players of 16 or older can do. Only then may your phone's advertising ID be used to show ads that fit your interests. You can switch them off again at any time in the same place, and reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Ad partners may process data in the United States and other countries; LevelPlay's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing; the game talks to it through Unity In-App Purchasing. We never see your card or bank details. We only learn which item was bought and its order number, so that we can give it to you, keep it from being given twice and bring it back with Restore purchases.

Why we use this data

To run the game and keep your farm safe (this is needed to give you the game you asked for). To show the ads you choose to watch, measure them and stop fraud (our legitimate interest in keeping the game free); personalised ads only with your consent, which you give and take back in Settings > Ad choices. To answer you when you write to us. We do not sell your data and we do not build a profile of you.

How long we keep it

The farm on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the game. The cloud copy and the random player number stay until you delete them or ask us to. Emails you send us are kept for up to two years.

Children

Farmly is suitable for everyone. On the first start the game asks for your age, and players under 16 never get personalised ads and choose their names from a ready-made list, so that they do not type personal details. Players under 13 have no friend code, no friends list and no farm card that others can see; their ads come only from ad networks that have certified to Google Play that they follow its Families policy, the ads are marked as being for a child, and the advertising ID is not used. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and think your child gave us personal data, write to us and we delete it.

Delete your data

In the game: Settings > Delete my data. This erases the farm on your phone, the copy in the cloud and the random player number, and the game starts from the beginning. Uninstalling the game removes only what is on the phone.

If the game is no longer installed: write to farmly.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your support code (Settings > Cloud save), or tell us that you connected Google Play Games. We delete the cloud copy within 30 days. Without the support code or a Google Play Games connection we cannot tell which farm is yours, because we do not know who you are.

Your rights

You can ask us what we store about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it is used. Write to farmly.support@gmail.com and we answer within 30 days. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is shown here and in the game with a new date.
